Strict Dominance

A strategy $s_i$ strictly dominates another strategy $s'_i$ for player $i$ if, for every profile $\sigma$ of the other players, playing $s_i$ yields strictly higher payoff:

$$\forall \sigma, \quad u_i(\sigma[i \mapsto s'_i]) < u_i(\sigma[i \mapsto s_i]).$$
