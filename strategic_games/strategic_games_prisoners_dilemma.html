Prisoner's Dilemma

The Prisoner's Dilemma is a two-player strategic game where each player can Cooperate ($C$) or Defect ($D$). The payoff matrix is:

$C$ $D$
$C$ (2, 2) (0, 3)
$D$ (3, 0) (1, 1)

Defecting is a weakly dominant strategy for each player. The unique Nash equilibrium is $(D, D)$ with payoff $(1, 1)$, which is Pareto-dominated by $(C, C)$ with payoff $(2, 2)$.
